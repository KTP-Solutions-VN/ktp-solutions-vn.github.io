Đội XeFleet Management · Prototype v0.1

Fleet management and per-trip profit and loss for trucking companies with 10–100 vehicles

Click-through prototype for usability testing, Round 2. Sample data (HCMC branch, 12/11/2026), not real data. Each screen has a State bar in the bottom-left corner for viewing empty, loading, error… states. Switch roles in the top-right corner of each screen.
