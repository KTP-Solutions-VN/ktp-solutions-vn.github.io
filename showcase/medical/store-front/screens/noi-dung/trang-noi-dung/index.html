What to expect at your first hospital tour

Published 10 September 2026 · CareJourney Care Team

Visiting Meridian Women's Clinic for the first time can feel like a big step in your pregnancy journey — here's exactly what to expect so you can walk in feeling prepared.

Before you arrive

Book your free slot through CareJourney and you'll receive a confirmation with the exact meeting point on Level 3. Tours run in small groups of up to 20 guests, guided by one of our nurse specialists.

During the tour

You'll walk through the labour ward, delivery suites and postnatal rooms, with plenty of time for questions. Most guests find it helpful to bring their birth partner along.

After the tour

Many parents choose to browse our Prenatal Wellness Packages right after the tour — you can add one to your cart directly from your phone while still on-site, and it will be grouped separately in your cart as a "Hospital Tour Visit" item, fulfilled through your next scheduled appointment.
