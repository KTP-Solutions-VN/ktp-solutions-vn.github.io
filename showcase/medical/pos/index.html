CareJourney POS Prototype

A dedicated touchscreen terminal for cashiers/pharmacists at Meridian Health retail & pharmacy outlets — no login, no dashboard, no sidebar. Start the linear sales → prescription → counseling → session-closing flow below.

#54 · Start here POS Sales Screen Touch-optimized sale screen — barcode scan, cart, payment methods, supervisor price-override authorization. #55 Patient Demographic & Clinical Info Pop-up form collecting NEHR-ready patient data and known allergies before a prescription item is dispensed. #56 Prescription Recording Records prescribed / dispensed / remaining quantity and derives Partial vs Full dispensing status. #57 Counseling Documentation Counseling checklist + notes; completes with a live NEHR submission and audit-trail confirmation. #58 Intervention Recording Logs an issue found during counseling and the action taken; loops back to Counseling when saved. #59 Cashier Session Closing Session KPIs, cash reconciliation, and the full Supervisor Overrides audit-trail panel for the shift. #60 POS Operational Reports Daily sales summary, session reports, detailed transactions, medication trends, NEHR submission log.
