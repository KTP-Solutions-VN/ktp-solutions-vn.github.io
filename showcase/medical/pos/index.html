CareJourney POS Prototype

A dedicated touchscreen terminal for cashiers/pharmacists at Meridian Health retail & pharmacy outlets — no login, no dashboard, no sidebar. Start the linear sale → dispensing → counseling → shift close-out flow below.

#1 · Start here Sales Terminal Touch-first checkout — barcode scanning, basket, payment options and supervisor-approved price overrides. #2 Patient Intake Pop-up capturing patient details and known allergies before any prescription item is dispensed. #3 Dispensing Record Tracks prescribed, dispensed and outstanding quantities and flags partial or complete supply. #4 Medication Counseling Counseling checklist and notes, finished with a health-record submission and audit confirmation. #5 Clinical Intervention Captures a problem found during counseling and the action taken, then returns to counseling. #6 Shift Close-Out Shift totals, cash count reconciliation and every supervisor override logged during the shift. #7 Pharmacy Reports Day-end sales, shift close-outs, transaction log, dispensing trends and health-record uploads.
